About This Tool

Image Watermark Tool is a modern online utility designed to help photographers, bloggers, creators, and businesses protect their images with stylish watermarks.

You can upload any image, add custom watermark text, adjust the font size, change opacity, and download the final image instantly.

The tool works directly in your browser using HTML5 Canvas technology, which means your images stay private and are never uploaded to any server.
