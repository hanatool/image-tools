About Image Metadata Cleaner

Image metadata often contains hidden information such as GPS location, camera model, device details, timestamps, and editing history. Sharing photos online without removing metadata may expose private data.

This free online Image Metadata Cleaner helps you remove EXIF and metadata directly in your browser. No registration, no uploads, and no tracking. Your files remain private while giving you a cleaner and safer image ready for social media, websites, or personal sharing.
